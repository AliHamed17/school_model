🛡️ Your mission

Scammers are flooding the school inbox with fake prizes and password traps. You are the Inbox Defender. You will teach an AI to read messages, block the scams and let the real ones through. Then you will fight the sneaky scammers who keep inventing new tricks.

The six levels

1Teach the guard▶ Watch this level 0:00

Do this
  • Read the red (scam) and green (safe) example messages.
  • Write one scam and one safe message of your own and add them.
  • Press Train.
Notice thisThe AI only knows what examples you give it.

2What did the AI learn?▶ Watch this level 0:37

Do this
  • Look at the strongest scam clues and safe clues.
  • Type a word in the search box (try free, win, now).
Notice thisWords that appear in both kinds of messages give weak clues.

3The inbox race▶ Watch this level 0:57

Do this
  • For each new message press Scam or Safe BEFORE the AI answers.
  • Read the coloured words: red pushes to scam, green pushes to safe.
Notice thisWho gets more right — you or the AI?

4Boss: sneaky scammers▶ Watch this level 1:28

Do this
  • Beat the tricky messages: misspellings, polite words, real messages that sound scary.
  • When the AI is wrong press “Teach the AI this message”.
Notice thisTeaching one message fixes only that message — scammers invent new tricks.

5Tune the guard▶ Watch this level 2:11

Do this
  • Move the slider: how sure must the AI be before it blocks?
  • Find a setting that catches at least 85% of scams and wrongly blocks at most 10% of real messages.
Notice thisCatching more scams usually blocks more real messages. There is no perfect setting.

6Report card▶ Watch this level 2:39

Do this
  • Check your badges.
  • Download the results file and open it in Excel. Chart it (Phase 10!).
Notice thisWhich badge was hardest to earn?

🕵️ Spot a real scam: 5 clues

  1. Pressure: “urgent”, “last warning”, “today only”.
  2. It asks for a password, a bank number or a code. Real companies never do.
  3. A prize you never entered for, or money you did not expect.
  4. Strange links, odd spelling, letters replaced by numbers or dots.
  5. Unsure? Do not click. Show a parent or teacher.

⭐ Challenges for fast finishers

  1. Write the sneakiest scam you can that still fools your trained AI. Then teach it. Does a similar one still fool it?
  2. Add 5 more scam and 5 more safe messages of your own. Does the Level 3 score improve?
  3. Switch language and play again. Why might an AI trained on English not work on Arabic or Hebrew?

Something went wrong?

The Train button does nothing new

Add your own messages first, then press Train again.

A level is locked

Finish the level before it. The numbers at the top turn green when a level is done.

The download did not start

Press the button again and allow downloads. The file opens in Excel.

My own messages vanished

They are kept only until you press “Play again” or change the language.
