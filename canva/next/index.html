Pick what to make from your design

1. What is your design about?

One short line. We use it to write ready-made prompts for you. Do not type your full name, phone number, address or school.

2. Bring your design along (optional)

  1. Open your design (button above) and click on the page.
  2. Press Ctrl+A to select everything, then Ctrl+C to copy.
  3. Open a tool below, then press Ctrl+V inside Canva to paste your design into the new one.

Copy and paste works between Canva designs in the same browser. Your original design stays as it is.

3. Pick what to make

Click a tool. Your prompt is copied for you and the tool opens in a new tab. Paste the prompt there with Ctrl+V and press Enter.

Before you start

  • Free to start: each tool has a free version with limits (for example a few AI pictures a day). If a tool asks you to pay, close it and choose another.
  • Accounts: Canva and Google tools use your Google sign-in. Microsoft and Adobe tools need their own free account. Some tools need you to be 13 or older. If you are younger, ask your teacher first.
  • Be careful: AI makes mistakes. Do not upload photos of people or put personal details in a prompt.
These are other companies' websites with their own rules and limits. This page only opens them and copies your prompt. Nothing you type here is sent anywhere.
