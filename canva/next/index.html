1. What is your design about?

One short line. We use it to write ready-made prompts for you. Do not type your full name, phone number, address or school.

2. Move your Canva design into another tool

  1. Download it from Canva. Click Open my Canva design above. If Canva asks you to sign in, click Continue with Google. Then choose File → Download → PNG → Download.
  2. Choose the file here. The picture stays on your computer. This page does not upload it.
  3. Pick where to take it. The buttons below wake up when your picture is ready.

Make a website from my picture

You get one file, my-website.html. Open it in your browser to see your website. Do not put your full name, phone number or school in it.
Moving into another Canva design instead?
  1. Open your design and click on the page. Press Ctrl+A to select everything, then Ctrl+C to copy.
  2. Open a Canva tool below, then press Ctrl+V to paste your design into the new one.

This works between Canva designs in the same browser. Your original design stays as it is.

3. Or start something new

Click a tool. Your prompt is copied for you and the tool opens in a new tab. Paste the prompt there with Ctrl+V and press Enter.

Before you start

  • Free to start: each tool has a free version with limits (for example a few AI pictures a day). If a tool asks you to pay, close it and choose another.
  • Accounts: Canva and Google tools use your Google sign-in. Microsoft and Adobe tools need their own free account. Some tools need you to be 13 or older. If you are younger, ask your teacher first.
  • Ads: a few free tools (like Photopea and Pixlr) show ads. Do not click them.
  • Be careful: AI makes mistakes. Do not upload photos of people or put personal details in a prompt.
