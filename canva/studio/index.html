Build a website with Canva Code

Describe the website you want to Canva's AI. Canva builds it. Publish it, then share your link with the class.

1

Open Canva Code

Canva Code needs a free Canva account. Click Open Canva, then Continue with Google and choose your Google account. You only do this once; Canva remembers you next time.

Open Canva ↗

On the Canva home page, click Code.

2

Describe your website

Type your idea in the Canva AI tab. A great prompt says:

  • Who it is for (classmates, my family, visitors)
  • What sections you want (about me, photos, quiz, table)
  • How it should look (colors, fun or serious, big title)
Need an idea? Copy one and paste it into Canva:
3

Improve it, one change at a time

Look at the preview. Then ask for one change, for example "make the title bigger" or "add a quiz at the bottom". Ask Canva AI to use the colors from your Canva design.

The AI makes mistakes. Check the facts and the spelling before you publish.

4

Publish and copy your link

Click Publish (top right in Canva), choose a short name for your website address, and publish. Canva shows you a link. Copy it.

Anyone with the link can see your website. Do not put your full name, phone number, address, school, or photos of people in it.

5

Share it with the class

Copy the link Canva showed you after publishing, then click Paste link.
