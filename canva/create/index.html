Before you start

  • Free to start: every tool here has a free version with limits. If a tool asks you to pay, close it and use the next one on the list.
  • Accounts: most tools use your Google sign-in. A few need their own free account. Never share your password.
  • Age: some tools are only for adults (marked 18+) and a few need a parent's OK. They are kept in the "More tools" fold. If a tool says you are too young, close it and use the next one.
  • Blocked or "not available for your account"? A school account can block a tool. Try your personal Google account, or use the next tool.
  • Be careful: AI makes mistakes, so check the facts. Do not upload photos of people or put personal details in a prompt or a website.
